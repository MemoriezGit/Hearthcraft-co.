Help & Backups

How to save a recipe

1
Take a picture

Tap Take Recipe Photo and photograph the handwritten or printed recipe.

2
Name it

Type a name you will remember, like Banana Bread.

3
Save and find it later

Tap Save This Recipe. The search and categories make finding it easy.

Keep your recipes safe

Recipes are kept privately on this iPhone, not on a paid website or a shared account. They will not automatically appear on another device. Export a backup to Files/iCloud Drive every so often, especially before replacing or resetting your phone.

Make a Backup

Saves all your recipes and photos into one backup file. Choose Save to Files and keep it in iCloud Drive if you use iCloud.

Restore Recipes

If you have a backup from this app, select the file to restore your cookbook.

Put the book on your Home Screen

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open the new Recipe Box icon whenever you want to cook. Internet is needed the first time you open this site; afterward the app may work offline if added to your Home Screen.
